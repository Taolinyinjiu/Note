# Introduction

> PX4 is the Professional Autopilot. Developed by world-class developers from industry and academia, and supported by an active world wide community, it powers all kinds of vehicles from racing and cargo drones through to ground vehicles and submersibles.
>
> PX4是专业自动驾驶仪。它由来自工业界和学术界的世界级开发人员开发，并得到活跃的全球社区的支持，为各种车辆提供动力，从赛车和货运无人机到地面车辆和潜水器。
> 
> This guide contains everything you need to assemble, configure, and safely fly a PX4-based vehicle. 
>
> 本指南包含组装，配置和安全飞行px4为基础的车辆所需的一切。

## How Do I Get Started?

[Getting Started](https://docs.px4.io/v1.15/en/getting_started/)should be read by all users! It provides an overview of PX4, including features provided by the flight stack (flight modes and safety features) and the supported hardware (flight controller, vehicles, airframes, telemetry systems, RC control systems).

所有用户都应该阅读 [Getting Started](https://docs.px4.io/v1.15/en/getting_started/)！它提供了PX4的概述，包括飞行堆栈（飞行模式和安全功能）和支持的硬件（飞行控制器，车辆，机身，遥测系统，RC控制系统）提供的功能。
